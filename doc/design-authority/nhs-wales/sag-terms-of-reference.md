# SAG Terms of Reference

## Introduction

The NHS Wales Standards and Architecture Group (SAG) provides strategic
direction, guidance, and oversight for the development, implementation, and
maintenance of architectural frameworks and standards across NHS Wales. SAG is
the national body with the authority to approve national architecture
principles, standards, and decisions.

## Objectives

* Discuss, agree, and approve national architecture principles, standards,
  and decisions for adoption across NHS Wales.
* Commission and oversee working groups and task and finish groups to develop
  standards and architecture artefacts on its behalf.
* Provide an escalation route for issues raised by its working groups.
* Ensure that national architecture and standards align with Health and Care
  policy and strategy.

## Working Groups

SAG is supported by working groups that develop and recommend standards for
approval. For example, the Wales Interoperability Standards Working Group:

* Discusses and agrees Health and Care interoperability standards for Wales
  (e.g. HL7 FHIR), collaborating with clinical, technical, and information
  standards subject matter experts.
* Reports directly to SAG, with one of its co-chairs representing the group
  at SAG meetings as an advisory, non-voting member.

## Governance

Organisations across NHS Wales - for example [DHCW](../dhcw/index.md) through its
[Technical Design Authority](../dhcw/tda-terms-of-reference.md) - adopt
SAG-approved national principles and standards, supplementing them with their
own local rationale, implications, and governance.
